Summary

Kite Rush does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your progress is stored on your device. If ads are enabled, our ad partner (Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (scores, coins, unlocked items, settings, missions, daily streak) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us unless you use the features below. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can export or import a save code from Settings.

Leaderboards (optional)

If you use online leaderboards, we store the nickname you choose, a random anonymous player ID generated by the game, and your scores. Nicknames are public. On iOS/Android, Game Center or Google Play Games may also be used under Apple's or Google's own privacy policies. To delete your leaderboard entries, email us your nickname.

Gameplay analytics (if enabled)

We may collect anonymous gameplay events (for example: a run started or ended, score, zone reached, which item was bought) to improve difficulty and fix problems. These events are not linked to your identity and do not include advertising identifiers.

Advertising

The mobile app may show ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads (for example, to continue a run or double coins). AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad-interaction data. On iOS you are asked for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency); in the EEA/UK a consent form is shown. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's advertising policies.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation of which item was purchased so we can deliver it.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules reminders (for example, your daily gift) locally on your device. No push token is sent to us.

Web versions

When played on game portals (such as Poki or CrazyGames), the portal's own privacy policy also applies to ads and analytics they run.

Children

Kite Rush is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or Singapore PDPA), you may request access to or deletion of data we hold about you. Contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.
